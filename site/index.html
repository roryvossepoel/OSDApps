How it works

From Windows installation to OOBE.

One flow. Your apps and configuration, applied before OOBE.

  1. 01

    OSDCloud · WinPE

    Install Windows

    Let OSDCloud apply Windows and complete its workflow.

  2. 02

    OSDStage · WinPE

    Stage your choices

    Stage apps and configuration, then validate. Connect an OSDCloud-labeled USB drive to automatically cache supported content for reuse.

  3. 03

    Restart → SetupComplete

    Apply automatically

    Restart into Windows. SetupComplete installs your apps and applies configuration before OOBE.

  4. 04

    OOBE

    Continue setup

    Review your delivery report, then continue with Windows setup or your Autopilot enrollment.
